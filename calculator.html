Annual CO₂
Sequestration
••••
kg/year
This metric estimates the amount of carbon dioxide absorbed from the atmosphere annually. The value of 21.8 kg is a widely used average for the annual sequestration rate of a mature tree, derived from forestry research.
🌿
Biodiversity
Potential Index
••••
This is a relative score indicating the complexity of the habitat. A higher variety of species in a given area generally supports a richer ecosystem. This index provides a simple measure of this potential.
🌡️
Localized
Cooling Effect
••••
°C
This estimates the potential reduction in local ambient air temperature. Urban green spaces can lower temperatures by 0.7°C to 1.3°C compared to open areas. This formula uses a conservative estimate based on an average reduction of 0.2°C per 100 m² of canopy cover.
💨
Air Quality
Improvement
••••
kg/year
This calculates the estimated annual removal of harmful fine particulate matter (PM2.5) from the air. Large, healthy trees can remove significantly more pollution than smaller trees; this formula uses a conservative average based on urban forestry studies.
💧
Stormwater
Runoff Reduction
••••
L/year
This metric estimates the volume of rainwater intercepted by vegetation, which reduces the load on drainage systems. The value is based on an average interception rate of 100 litres per square meter of green space, derived from hydrological models.
⭐
Green
Branding Score
••••
A proprietary, motivational score that reflects the scale, complexity, and maturity of the greening initiative. The logarithmic scale indicates that while all contributions are valuable, sustained and diverse projects have a greater cumulative impact.
